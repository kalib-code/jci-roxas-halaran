Frequently Asked Questions

What is JCI?

JCI (Junior Chamber International) is a global organization of young active citizens committed to developing leadership skills and making positive change in communities.

Who can join JCI?

Membership is open to young people aged 18 to 40 who are interested in leadership, personal growth, and community service.

How do I become a member?

You can join by reaching out to our chapter through our contact page or attending one of our events to learn more about the application process.

What are the benefits of joining JCI?

Members gain access to leadership training, networking opportunities, community projects, and personal development programs.

Is there a membership fee?

Yes, there is an annual membership fee which helps fund chapter activities and programs. Please contact us for current rates.

What projects does JCI organize?

We organize a variety of projects including leadership seminars, community service initiatives, youth development programs, and international exchanges.

How can I participate?

Follow our website and social media pages for event announcements, or contact us directly to get involved.

Can I join without leadership experience?

Absolutely! JCI is designed to help members develop leadership skills, regardless of previous experience.

How can businesses partner with JCI?

Contact us through our partnership inquiry form or email to discuss collaboration opportunities.
