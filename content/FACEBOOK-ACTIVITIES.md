# JCI Roxas Halaran activities from the Facebook page

Source: 875 posts captured from the chapter's Facebook page on 2026-10-21. Post numbers (`#n`) are the `order` field in the capture; #0 is the newest and #874 the oldest (January 2023).

How to read this record:
- Dates come from the captions. Where a caption gives no date, the date is inferred from the post's position and its neighbours and marked **(approx)**.
- Only what the posts say is recorded. Numbers appear only where a post states them.
- **Role** is what the posts say the chapter did: organizer, co-host, partner or participant.
- `seed: <slug>` marks a match with `PROJECTS` in `db/seed-data.js`.
- Several posts about one activity are grouped into one entry.
- Left out: birthdays, holiday greetings, member spotlights, other organizations' advisories and reposts, and generic motivational or awareness posts.
- Within each year, entries run from oldest to newest.
