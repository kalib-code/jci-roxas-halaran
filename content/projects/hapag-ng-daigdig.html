"111: Hapag ng Daigdig" ("The World's Table") is an original song and music video by JCI Roxas Halaran. The chapter released it in 2026 as its anthem for the 2026 JCI World Congress in the Philippines. It is a work, not an event: a song made to welcome the world.

The song

The track is Filipino Amapiano. It blends the log drum of the global genre with the chapter's local commitment to SDG 2: Zero Hunger. It also pays tribute to JCI's 111-year legacy, with the "111 steps" of discipline, leadership and institutional wisdom it takes to make sure no one is left behind at the world's table.

  • Executive producer: JCI Roxas Halaran
  • Production and sound design: Reverberate
  • Lyrics and creative direction: Joe, 2026 Deputy Secretary

The music video

The video, by Straylights, is about "The Hands That Lead": the quiet preparations, morning routines and "sunset sessions" where the chapter says the real vision is born. It features 2026 Local Organization President Jaime Altavas, Immediate Past President Lemuel Capa and JCI Senator JP Sorongon.

The chapter's message with it: "We don't just host; we build a place for every nation at our table."

Where it lives now

"Hapag ng Daigdig - 111" is one of the 13 tracks on the core album of the chapter's HALAD AUDIO LAB, the free audio library it shares with members and sister chapters.

See you at the World Congress. To be part of what comes next, join JCI Roxas Halaran.
