Igpat sa Dalanon was a tricycle lights and sound competition held during the Sinadya Festival 2024. It ran on the night of December 7, 2024, from 6:00 PM, at the Roxas City Bandstand.

The competition

Three teams brought their tricycles to the bandstand: Team Lakay Kitty, Team Laglag and Team Low Profile. The chapter described a night of dazzling lights, electrifying sounds and unforgettable performances.

The prizes:

  • PHP 5,000 for Best Sound in each category
  • PHP 5,000 for Best Light
  • PHP 500 and an event t-shirt for each of five consolation winners

Who ran it

JCI Roxas Halaran co-organized the event in close coordination with Councilor Jaime Altavas and Angkas, under the line "Angkas na! Upod kita!" The chapter credited the councilor's leadership and support for bringing the community together. He became the chapter's president in 2026.

The chapter thanked everyone who joined and congratulated all the winners and participants. The posts do not name the winning teams.

More Sinadya

The chapter was busy during the 2024 festival. That month it also held a Sinadya Celebrity Zumba Party, displayed artwork by Jonard Villarde at the Christmas Bazaar to raise funds for pediatric cancer patients, and staged The Nutcracker.
