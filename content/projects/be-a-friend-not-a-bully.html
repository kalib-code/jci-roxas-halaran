"Be a Friend, Not a Bully" is a special project of JCI Roxas Halaran for children. It teaches them how to be kind, respectful and caring to others, and reminds everyone that it is always better to make friends than to hurt others with words or actions.

How it works

The project uses fun games, stories and teamwork activities to help children learn to say no to bullying and yes to friendship. The idea is simple: when children choose to be friends, schools and communities become happier and safer places for everyone.

Part of Education for Peace

The chapter ran the project in 2025 under JCI Philippines' Education for Peace program, with the theme of empowering communities through education for peace. In May 2025 the chapter also co-hosted Education for Peace trainings with fellow JCI chapters.
