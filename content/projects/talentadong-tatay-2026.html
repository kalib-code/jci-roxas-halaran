Talentadong Tatay is a talent competition for fathers. Its second year ran on Father's Day, June 21, 2026, at the Events Area of SM City Roxas. It was presented by JCI Roxas Halaran with SM City Roxas under the chapter's Man-Up flagship.

How it worked

Fathers could sing, dance or show any other talent, either solo or with their family as a back-up crew. Families signed their dads up by messaging the chapter's Facebook page.

The competition ran right after the 2026 IRONMEN Awards, as the second half of the chapter's Father's Day activation. The afternoon moved from formal honors straight into performances. The goal was to give local fathers a community-wide stage and a chance to step out of the "hidden moments" of everyday fatherhood.
