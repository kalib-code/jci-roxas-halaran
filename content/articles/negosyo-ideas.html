The best negosyo ideas for the province solve a local need with skills and resources you already have. In a place like Roxas City, Capiz, strong options include seafood and dried fish processing, pasalubong and food products, home-based food businesses, repair and personal services, tourism services, and online selling of local goods. Below are 20 ideas grouped by type, with notes on starting capital, skills and permits, plus a simple way to test an idea before you spend your savings.

No list can tell you which business will work for you. Use these ideas as starting points, then talk to future customers before you commit. Capital levels are described as low, medium or high because actual costs vary a lot by location, scale and supplier.

How to choose from these negosyo ideas

Before you read the list, keep three questions in mind:

  • Who will buy, and how often? A product people need every week beats one they buy once a year.
  • What do you already have? A skill, a kitchen, a motorbike, a spot near a school or a family recipe can be your head start.
  • What makes yours different? If three people on your street already sell the same thing, you need a better product, better service or a better location.

Food and seafood negosyo ideas

Roxas City is known as the Seafood Capital of the Philippines, and food is one of the most natural places to start. Read more in our piece on the Seafood Capital of the Philippines.

1. Dried fish and seafood products

Tuyo, danggit, dried pusit and other dried seafood are pasalubong favorites. Work with local fisherfolk for supply, focus on cleanliness and good packaging, and sell to visitors and online buyers. Capital: low to medium. Processed food may need extra permits, so ask your city hall and the FDA about requirements before selling beyond your area.

2. Bottled or ready-to-eat seafood

Products like gourmet tuyo in oil, bagoong or seafood chili sauces travel well and fetch higher prices. Capital: medium. You will need a clean, consistent process and proper labeling.

3. Home-based baked goods

Breads, cakes, cookies and kakanin (native rice cakes) for birthdays, fiestas and offices. Start with pre-orders so you only bake what is sold. Capital: low.

4. Food stall or food cart

Near schools, terminals, markets or along the baywalk, a stall selling merienda (afternoon snacks), drinks or rice meals can do well. Location is everything. Capital: low to medium.

5. Packed meals and catering

Offices, events and families often need packed lunches or small-party catering. Begin with a short menu you can cook well and on time. Capital: medium.

Retail negosyo ideas

6. Sari-sari store

Still one of the most common negosyo ideas in the Philippines. It works best where there is no other store within easy walking distance. Track your stock and avoid too much pautang (credit to customers). Capital: low to medium.

7. Pasalubong shop

Gather local products, including dried seafood, delicacies and crafts, into one shop near where visitors pass. You can partner with small producers on consignment to lower your starting costs. Capital: medium.

8. Rice and grocery retail

Rice is a daily need. Selling rice, eggs and basic goods in a residential area offers steady, if thin, margins. Capital: medium.

9. Ukay-ukay or preloved selling

Preloved clothes, bags and shoes sell both in stalls and through Facebook live selling. Sorting good pieces is the real skill. Capital: low.

Service negosyo ideas

Services often need less capital than products because your main asset is your skill and time.

10. Laundry service

Wash-dry-fold services are popular with students, boarders and working families. Start with pick-up and delivery from home before renting a shop. Capital: medium to high.

11. Motorcycle and small engine repair

Motorcycles and tricycles are everywhere in the province. Trained mechanics with honest pricing build loyal customers fast. TESDA courses can help you get certified. Capital: medium.

12. Phone and gadget repair

Screen replacements, battery changes and basic troubleshooting are in steady demand. Capital: low to medium.

13. Printing, photocopy and school supplies

Near schools and government offices, printing, lamination, ID photos and school supplies are reliable sellers, especially at the start of the school year. Capital: medium.

14. Tutoring and review classes

If you are good at math, science, English or reading, tutoring children after school costs almost nothing to start. Capital: low.

15. Beauty and grooming services

Home-service haircuts, nails, makeup for events and barbering. Build your reputation with good photos and reviews. Capital: low to medium.

Tourism and creative negosyo ideas

16. Tour and boat-trip services

Visitors to Roxas City come for the beaches, islands and food. Guided food crawls, island-hopping tie-ups with licensed boat operators, or transport services can serve them. Work with the local tourism office and make safety your first priority. See our guide to Capiz tourist spots for ideas on where visitors go.

17. Capiz shell and native crafts

Capiz is famous for its shell craft. Lamps, windows, decor and souvenirs made by local artisans can be sold to visitors, online and through trade fairs. You can partner with existing craftspeople instead of starting production yourself. Learn more about Capiz shells. Capital: medium.

18. Photography, video and event styling

Weddings, debuts, baptisms and fiestas all need photos, videos and decor. Start with a good camera or phone, a portfolio of a few events and fair rates. Capital: medium.

Online and agri negosyo ideas

19. Online selling and social media services

Sell local products online, or offer services to other small businesses: managing Facebook pages, taking product photos, writing captions or setting up online shops. Many local negosyo owners want help but don't know where to find it. Capital: low.

20. Backyard farming: vegetables, poultry or mushrooms

If you have land, vegetables, native chicken, eggs or oyster mushrooms can supply your neighborhood, carinderias and markets. Ask your municipal agriculture office about training and support. Capital: low to medium.

Test your idea before you spend big

The most common mistake is spending all your savings on equipment before knowing whether anyone will buy. Try this instead:

  1. Make or offer a small batch. Ten jars, twenty boxes, five haircuts.
  2. Sell to real customers, not just family. Post in a local Facebook group or set up a small table at a bazaar.
  3. Ask for honest feedback. Price, taste, quality, packaging, service.
  4. Count your real costs. Include ingredients, packaging, transport, your time and losses.
  5. Repeat. If people come back and buy again without being asked, you have a negosyo worth growing.

Don't skip the permits

Once you're selling regularly, register properly. For most sole proprietors, that means DTI business name registration, a barangay clearance, a Mayor's Permit and BIR registration. Food, water and some other businesses need extra permits. Our DTI business registration guide explains the steps and fees as of 2026, and the official DTI Business Name Registration System is where you register online.

If you need help, the Negosyo Center or DTI provincial office in your area offers free business advice and training. For the bigger picture, read our guide to entrepreneurship in the Philippines.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best negosyo for beginners?

One that matches a skill you already have and needs little capital, like baked goods, tutoring, online selling or a small food stall. Start small, learn fast, then grow.

What negosyo can I start with small capital?

Home-based food, tutoring, ukay-ukay, phone repair (if you have the skill), social media services and backyard vegetables can all start with low capital.

Do I need to register a small home business?

Yes, once you are operating as a business. Register your business name with DTI, then get your barangay clearance, Mayor's Permit and BIR registration.

What negosyo ideas fit Roxas City?

Ideas built on what the area is known for: seafood processing, pasalubong, food services, Capiz shell crafts and tourism services, plus everyday services like repair, laundry and printing.
