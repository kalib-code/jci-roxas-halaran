# Before publishing: facts for the chapter to confirm

The writers checked dates, laws and processes against the web sources linked in each article. The items below are local details or claims they could not fully confirm. Articles are imported as drafts, so nothing goes live until an officer publishes it.

## Every article
- Hiligaynon words used (halad, sadya, namit, palihog, bulig-bulig, uga, panublion, baybay, kulba, kaisog, mamumugon, paghiliusa, pagtanum, "Salamat gid"): have a native speaker check meanings. "Malasakit" is Tagalog and is used that way.
- Chapter projects are mentioned by title (and date) only. Add what happened, partners and numbers where you want more.
- "Founded in 2023": confirm the charter date.

## Chapter
- **jci-roxas-halaran-2026-year-in-review**: written as of October 2026; refresh in December. Needs, for each 2026 project: what it was, venue, partners, participants, outcomes. Say whether SA DAGAT and Hapag ng Daigdig ran in 2026. Organizer or participant at Provincial Arbor Day 2026? Confirm sponsors are happy to be named. Add any 2026 awards.
- **what-is-jci-philippines**: check the JCI Creed against the official copy (quoted from memory; jci.cc blocks automated access). Confirm the awards wording (2024 Temiong and Quadro Top 5 nominee; Star Local President 2024 Sorongon, 2025 Capa). Add why the chapter is named "Halaran" if you want that story told.
- **sdg-philippines**: links projects to SDGs from their titles (Arbor Day → 13, 15; Men's Mental Health → 3; anti-bullying → 4, 16).

## Capiz
- **halaran-festival-guide**: sources disagree on when Sinadya and Halaran merged and split. The article says they were held together for years and the city now uses "Sinadya". Confirm.
- **roxas-city-capiz-guide**: population 179,292 (2020 census, 47 barangays) via PhilAtlas, not the PSA page.
- **typhoon-preparedness-checklist**: confirm the CERT hotline (036) 522-7878 and add CDRRMO/PDRRMO numbers.
- **capiz-tourist-spots**: Olotayan, Culajao and Suhot Cave details come from travel blogs; there are no fees or schedules.
- **capiz-history**: the 1716 date and "second Spanish settlement after Cebu" come from secondary sources. Capiz population 804,952 (2020); 2024 census may be 835,098.
- **capiz-shells**: no BFAR order number or size limit is cited.
- Panay church bell: "often called the largest in Asia" (reputation, not a confirmed ranking).

## Holidays
- **bonifacio-day**: exact death site in Maragondon is disputed; the article stays general.
- **araw-ng-kagitingan**: Death March numbers vary by source; the article says so.
- **labor-day-philippines**: the size of the 1903 march varies by source.
- **edsa-people-power-anniversary**: pay rules are left to DOLE's yearly advisory.
- No 2027 dates are stated; the 2026 dates come from Proclamation 1006.

## Business and community
- **dti-business-registration-guide**: fees ₱200/500/1,000/2,000 + ₱30 doc stamp, as of 2026. Confirm the Roxas City Mayor's Permit office and whether there is a Negosyo Center in the city.
- **entrepreneurship-in-the-philippines**: MSME share (99.63%) is the DTI summary of PSA 2023 data, checked through search snippets only.
- **ngo-youth-organizations-philippines**: NYC and SEC registration steps are general; check with the Capiz/Roxas City youth office.
- **tree-planting-philippines**: native species are examples only; it tells readers to ask DENR.
