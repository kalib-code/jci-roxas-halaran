## Contradictions between the Facebook posts and other articles

These were found by searching `content/articles/*.html` for chapter claims. The other articles were **not** edited; fix them, or confirm they are fine, before publishing.

1. **"Founded in 2023"** in **what-is-jci-philippines** ("a local organization of JCI Philippines based in Roxas City, Capiz, founded in 2023"). The posts confirm the March 18, 2023 affiliation but also describe a revival:
   - "one of the pioneer members who played a vital role in reviving JCI Roxas Halaran chapter 2 years ago" (#682, approx 2024)
   - Dr. Denev Alba "assisted President Jp during the revitalization of the Halaran chapter" (#672)
   - Fulbert Woo's "efforts in reviving JCI Roxas Halaran" (#359)
   - "our 2017 President, Pepe Del Rosario" (#494)
   - "Past President Hon. Nico Yap" and "Past President Atty. Earl Ross Judgee Del Castillo" (#840)
   - JP Sorongon signs as "JCI Roxas Halaran President 2022-2024" (#568)

   Suggested fix: "affiliated with JCI Philippines in March 2023". Ask whether to mention the chapter's earlier history.
2. **Website nomination credited to the Temiong Awards**, in **what-is-jci-philippines** ("a Top 5 nominee for Best Local Organization Website at the Temiong Awards"). The posts list it as a Quadro Awards nomination, Most Outstanding Local Organization Website (#617). The seed `AWARDS` row makes the same attribution (`awardedBy: "Temiong Awards"`).
3. **Missing award.** **what-is-jci-philippines** and the seed `AWARDS` omit the 2024 **Quadro Award for Best Educational Program (The Nutcracker)**. The chapter calls it its first Quadro Award (#617). They also omit the 2026 awards listed above.
4. **"Star Local Presidents by JCI Philippines"** in **what-is-jci-philippines**. Sorongon's award was given at the JCI World Congress in Taiwan (#585), and Capa's post thanks "JCI" (#411, #454). It is a small wording point; consider "by JCI".
5. **IRONMEN Awards 2025 date.** The seed `startDate` is `2025-07-08`, and the old year-in-review said 8 July 2025. The posts say it was **held July 5, 2025** at SM City Roxas (#420, #451). No other article states the date, but the project page will show the seed date.
6. **Projects the chapter supported but did not run, described as "our projects".**
   - **Provincial Arbor Day 2026** was led by CaPENRO, and the chapter joined (#127). It is called "our recent work" in **community-development-how-to-start** and "one of JCI Roxas Halaran's projects" in **tree-planting-philippines**.
   - **Sampukanay 2026** was organized by StreetKingz Fam - SJR, and the chapter supported it under Lupad Lokal (#103, #121). **volunteer-opportunities-philippines** lists it among "recent projects".
   - **Symposium on Men's Mental Health** was hosted by Leon Ganzon Polytechnic College, with the chapter as resource partner (#88). **what-is-jci-philippines** lists it under "Some of our projects so far".

   None of these is false, but the chapter may want "joined" or "supported" wording.
7. **Officer roles** (seed `BOARDS` 2026 and the `/officers` page, not an article). The chapter's 2026 board post (#362) gives:
   - Elmer Jimenez Jr.: **Auditor** (seed: Director)
   - Raymark Bigcas: **VP for Internal**; Jasper Sayo: **VP for External** (seed: "Vice President")
   - Judy Daliva: **Director for Membership**
   - Johbert Flores: **Director for Social Media & Marketing**
   - Eugenio Dalida Jr.: **Director for Internationalism**
   - Manuel Luis Luna: **Director for Community**
   - Jaime Miguel Dadivas is not on that list. Later posts call him "BOD Miggy Dadivas" (#175) and "RC Miggy Dadivas" (#198).
8. **Who can join.** Many articles invite "young Capiznons" or "young people aged 18 to 40" with no mention of gender. Examples: bonifacio-day, araw-ng-kagitingan, capiz-history, entrepreneurship-in-the-philippines, negosyo-ideas, labor-day-philippines, rizal-day. The seed FAQ says "Anyone aged 18 to 40". The posts describe "an all-male chapter" (#130, #182) and OTIs "open to young men from 18 to 40" (#576). Confirm the intended wording.
9. **"Be a Friend, Not a Bully" launch date.** **anti-bullying-act-philippines** says it launched "in July 2025", and the seed date is 2025-07-04. The only post about it (#399) has no date, so this can be neither confirmed nor contradicted.
